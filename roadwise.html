    How the forecast works and where the numbers come from
    1. Each day starts from the route's normal week, scaled so an average day is 100%.
    2. Holiday factors multiply that. Factors tagged Records line up with peak days SANRAL and the N3 Toll Concession have published for past seasons. Factors tagged Estimate are reasonable guesses not yet checked against counts.
    3. Some routes swing harder than others. SANRAL has listed the N12 and N14 as free-flowing for a whole festive season, while the N3 has reached 2,000–3,500 vehicles an hour on peak days.
    4. Extra driving time only starts once traffic is well above average and is an estimate.

    Sources used:

      Version 2, sample model. I couldn't find public day-by-day traffic counts, so the normal-week shape is still an estimate. SANRAL keeps continuous count stations on these routes, and getting that data is the next step to back it up. School dates are approximate.
